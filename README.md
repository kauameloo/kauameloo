### About

I got into tech wanting to be a developer. I took the Systems Development technical program at SENAI and graduated in Systems Analysis and Development at FIAP, building a foundation in back end, front end and databases.

The first turn came with **Cloud Security**: identity and access management, service identities for integrating on-premise applications with cloud resources, and compliance monitoring across Azure and AWS. That is where security stopped being a side topic and became the topic.

The second turn brought me to **CSIRT / DFIR**, where I work today: detection, triage and incident response, threat hunting and analysis of malicious artifacts.

The development background did not become the past. It is what lets me read an artifact's code, automate evidence collection and understand from the inside the application I am defending.

---

### What I Do

**Incident Response**
- Security incident response and management
- Cyber threat hunting driven by the MITRE ATT&CK and PEAK frameworks
- Malware and malicious artifact analysis and investigation
- Threat investigation involving phishing and adversarial techniques
- Threat mapping through the Cyber Kill Chain and Fraud Kill Chain
- Development of incident response playbooks and runbooks

**Detection & Monitoring**
- SIEM use cases and dashboards built with SPL queries
- Log analysis across Firewall, WAF, Proxy, IPS and Endpoint
- Cloud compliance and security posture monitoring

**Cloud & Identity**
- Access management (IAM), service identities and access groups
- Multi-cloud environments: Azure and AWS
- Compliance audits
